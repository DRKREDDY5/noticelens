# Understanding your CP01C notice

Source: https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-cp01c-notice

## What this notice is about

We verified your identity theft claim and found no tax-related fraudulent activity. We placed an identity theft indicator on your tax account and will monitor it to help prevent future fraud.

## What you need to do

- File your federal tax returns on time.
- Pay any tax you owe by the due date to avoid interest and penalties.
- View your [payment options](https://www.irs.gov/payments) or [set up a payment plan](https://www.irs.gov/payments/online-payment-agreement-application) if you can’t pay in full.

## What you need to know

- You can request an [Identity Protection Personal Identification Number (IP PIN)](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin) for added account protection by creating an account online.
- Learn how to protect yourself from identity theft and report it to law enforcement, credit bureaus, and financial institutions.
- Visit our [Identity theft Central](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-central) to learn more on reporting identity theft to law enforcement. You can also find information for protecting yourself, and keeping your personal information safe, including your children’s taxpayer identification numbers, identity documents, benefits, and other identity information.

## Frequently asked questions

#### What does the Identity Theft Indicator mean for my account? (updated Nov. 13, 2025)

The indicator on your account doesn’t affect your ability to file your tax return, make payments, or get a refund Whenever your account has the identity theft indicator, you’ll need to use an Identity Protection Personal Identification Number (IPPIN) when you file your tax returns.

#### How long will the indicator stay on my account? (updated Nov. 13, 2025)

The indicator will stay on your account until you specifically request that we remove it.

#### Who can I contact if I have more questions about identity theft? (updated Nov. 13, 2025)

You can contact us at [800-908-4490](tel:800-908-4490) for assistance with identity theft-related issues.

#### Who can I contact for help with other tax account issues? (added Nov. 13, 2025)

You can visit our [Tools](https://www.irs.gov/help/tools) page or call the IRS help desk at [800-829-1040](tel:800-829-1040).

#### Where can I find more information on preventing identity theft? (added Nov. 13, 2025)

You can find it by visiting:

- [Federal Trade Commission (FTC)](https://consumer.ftc.gov/features/identity-theft) for consumer information and their online [Privacy and Security](https://consumer.ftc.gov/identity-theft-and-online-security/online-privacy-and-security).
- [Social Security Administration (SSA)](https://www.ssa.gov/fraud/).
- [Taxes. Security. Together](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/taxes-security-together) for tips on keeping your personal, financial, and tax data safer.

## Tips for next year

- [File](https://www.irs.gov/filing) your tax return on time. If you can’t file on time, e-file [Form 4868](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-4868) to request an automatic extension of time to file. An extension to file doesn’t extend the time you have to pay.
- [Pay](https://www.irs.gov/payments) any taxes you owe by the due date to avoid interest and penalties, or explore alternate options like a [payment plan](https://www.irs.gov/payments/online-payment-agreement-application) if you can’t pay in full.
- Learn more about how to [get an identity protection PIN (IP PIN)](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin).

### Need help?

- [You can authorize someone](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/power-of-attorney-and-other-authorizations) to represent you before the IRS or inspect and/or receive confidential tax information.
- You may be eligible for free help from the [Taxpayer Advocate Service (TAS)](https://www.taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/contact-us/#FindlocalTAS).
- You may qualify for help from a [Low Income Taxpayer Clinic](https://www.taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/about-us/low-income-taxpayer-clinics-litc/).

- You can call the telephone number on your notice or letter. If you didn’t receive a letter or notice, use [Let us help you](https://www.irs.gov/help/let-us-help-you#IRSphonenumbers).
